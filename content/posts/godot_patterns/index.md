This is the index page for my posts on software design patterns for [Godot 4](https://godotengine.org/), and how I personally understand and use them in my projects.

A large basis of my understanding for the design patterns stems from the original [Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software](https://www.amazon.com/Design-Patterns-Elements-Reusable-Object-Oriented/dp/0201633612) by the Gang of Four, but contextualized for video game development, my main source of inspiration comes from [Game Programming Patterns](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/) by Robert Nystrom.

This part of my website is essentially for documenting my understanding behind some of the rationale behind using these design patterns in game development, while contextualizing their use in Godot 4. I may not have posts for all design patterns either due to not authoring the pages for them yet, or due to not specifically finding a use or justification for the pattern, but as I continue to write posts, I will update the list below with links to their pages. You can also check the contents below the list for a chronological list of posts relating to this topic.
